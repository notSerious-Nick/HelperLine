How Support Moves Forward

Helper once used “helper engines” to guide trains through steep canyon routes. Today, The Helper Line helps artists find their way forward through mentorship, exhibition opportunities, student involvement, and family art experiences. Support moves forward.

  1. Guest Artists

    Mentor emerging artists through talks, feedback, and portfolio reviews.

  2. Emerging Artists

    Gain exhibition space, artwork sales opportunities, and promotional visibility.

  3. Art Students

    Build portfolios and professional networks while supporting public art and youth workshops.

  4. Children & Families

    Join hands-on art experiences that make creativity accessible and help sustain the program.

  5. The Helper Line Program

    Workshop participation and local partnerships help fund the next artist opportunity.

In Helper, support does not stop with one artist—it moves forward to the next generation.
